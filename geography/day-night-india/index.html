📘 Learn the science

💡 The big idea

The Earth spins on its axis once a day, from west to east. This spinning is called rotation. At any moment the half of the Earth facing the Sun has day and the other half has night. The line between them is the circle of illumination. Because the Earth turns towards the east, places in the east see the Sun first. India is about 2 950 km wide from west to east (from about 68° E to 97° E), so the Sun rises in Arunachal Pradesh and Assam nearly two hours before it rises in Gujarat. Yet the whole country uses one clock time, Indian Standard Time (IST), set for the 82.5° E line of longitude that passes near Mirzapur in Uttar Pradesh.

🧮 Key formulas & rules

360° in 24 h → 15° per hour → 1° of longitude = 4 minutes India's width ≈ 97° − 68° = 29° → 29 × 4 min ≈ 2 hours cos H = (sin(−0.83°) − sin φ · sin δ) ÷ (cos φ · cos δ)

φ = latitude of the place, δ = latitude where the Sun is overhead that day, H = half the length of the day in degrees (÷ 15 gives hours)

  • The −0.83° is there because sunrise is counted when the top edge of the Sun appears, and air bends sunlight a little. That is why even on the equinox the day is about 12 h 6 min, not exactly 12 h.
  • The simulation also adds the equation of time: the Sun runs up to about 16 minutes fast or slow compared with a clock during the year, because the Earth's orbit is an ellipse and its axis is tilted.

Simplification: the Earth is drawn from above the North Pole with India as a simple patch between 8° N and 37° N and 68° E and 97° E, not its real outline. Hills and buildings, which hide the real sunrise, are ignored. Times match real almanacs to within a couple of minutes.

🌍 In real life

  • Tea gardens in Assam traditionally keep their own "chai bagan time", about one hour ahead of IST, to use the early daylight.
  • In June the Sun rises in Dibrugarh a little after 4:15 am but in Dwarka only after 6 am, so school timings differ from state to state.
  • India uses one time zone so that trains, banks and offices across the country run on the same clock.

📖 Key terms

  • Rotation: the spinning of the Earth on its axis, once every 24 hours, from west to east.
  • Axis: the imaginary line through the North and South Poles that the Earth spins around.
  • Longitude: the angle east or west of the Prime Meridian at Greenwich; lines of longitude run from pole to pole.
  • Indian Standard Time (IST): the single clock time used across India, based on 82.5° E and 5 h 30 min ahead of Greenwich.
