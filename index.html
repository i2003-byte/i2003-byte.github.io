    How it works

    Three steps to “aha!”

    🧭

    Pick a topic

    Browse by subject, level or search. Every simulation opens instantly — no sign-up, no install.

    🎛️

    Play with the controls

    Drag sliders, press play, pause and step. Watch the numbers and graphs change live.

    💡

    Understand the concept

    Open the Learn panel for the big idea, key formulas, real-world examples and challenges.

    For teachers & students

    Made for the classroom — and the bedroom desk

    👩‍🏫 For teachers

    • 🖥️Fullscreen mode for the projector or smartboard.
    • 🔗Shareable setups: “Share link” saves every slider in the URL, so the whole class opens the exact same experiment.
    • 🎯Ready-made challenges in every Learn panel for discussion or homework.

    🧑‍🎓 For students

    • 📚Self-study: see the formulas from your textbook come alive.
    • 📝Homework exploration: test “what if?” questions and check your answers.
    • 📱Works everywhere: phone, tablet or laptop — touch-friendly controls.
