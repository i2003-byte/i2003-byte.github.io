    How it works

    Three steps to “aha!”

    🧭

    Pick a topic

    Browse by subject, level or search. Every simulation opens instantly — no sign-up, no install.

    🎛️

    Play with the controls

    Drag sliders, press play, pause and step. Watch the numbers and graphs change live.

    Open the Learn panel for the big idea, key formulas, real-world examples and challenges.

    • 📚Self-study: see the formulas from your textbook come alive.
