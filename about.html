About

Science is something you do.

SimLab is a free, open collection of interactive simulations. Every experiment runs right in your browser — on a phone, a tablet or a classroom projector — with no sign-up and no installs.

Hands-on

Shareable

Accessible

Keyboard friendly, screen-reader labels, reduced-motion support and light/dark themes.

What’s inside

We’re starting with Physics, including the Sound Lab — 14 simulations covering the Class 7 chapter on sound: how sound is made and travels, amplitude and loudness, frequency and pitch, the voice box and the ear, the range of hearing, music versus noise, noise pollution, and two instruments you can build and play. Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology and Astronomy are on the way.

How it’s built

Plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks and no build step. The whole site is driven by one data file, catalog.js: add an entry there and the menus, search, subject pages and sitemap update themselves. The source is on GitHub and the README explains how to add new subjects and simulations.

A note on accuracy

Simulations simplify the real world so ideas are easy to see — for example, vibrations are slowed down and air resistance is often ignored. The Learn panel of each simulation explains the model it uses. Hearing ranges and decibel levels are typical values; real measurements vary.
