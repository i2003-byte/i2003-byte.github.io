📘 Learn the science

💡 The big idea

Draw any triangle at all: thin, fat, or with a wide obtuse corner. Its three angles always add up to 180°, the same as a straight line.

You can see this by tearing off the three corners and placing them side by side: they fit exactly along a straight line. A line drawn through the top corner parallel to the base explains why, using the alternate angles you met with parallel lines.

🧮 Key rules

∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°

Angle sum property. So if you know two angles, the third is 180° minus their sum.

Exterior angle = sum of the two interior opposite angles

Extend side AB beyond B to D. Then ∠CBD = 180° − ∠B = ∠A + ∠C.

Why (parallel-line proof): draw a line through C parallel to AB. The angle on its left next to C equals ∠A and the angle on its right equals ∠B (alternate interior angles). Those two angles and ∠C sit together on a straight line, so they add up to 180°.

A triangle can have at most one right angle or one obtuse angle. With all three angles below 90° it is acute.

🌍 Real-world examples

  • Roof trusses and bridge frames use triangles. If the slope of a roof is 30° at each side, the angle at the top must be 120°.
  • A set square has angles 30°, 60°, 90°; the other one has 45°, 45°, 90°. Both add up to 180°.
  • Surveyors measure two angles of a triangle and work out the third, instead of measuring it.

🎯 Try this

  1. Tap “Tear off the corners”. Then drag corner C to make a very different triangle and tear them again. Do the corners still fill the straight line?
    Show answerYes, every time. Whatever the shape, the three corners together make exactly 180°.
  2. Set ∠A = 90° and ∠B = 45°. What is ∠C, and what kind of triangle is it?
    Show answer∠C = 180° − 90° − 45° = 45°. It is a right-angled isosceles triangle, like one of your set squares.
  3. Can you make a triangle with two right angles? Try to set ∠A = 90° and ∠B = 90°.
    Show answerNo. 90° + 90° already makes 180°, leaving nothing for ∠C: the two sides would be parallel and never meet. The sim will not let both be 90°.
  4. Choose “The exterior angle at B” with ∠A = 50° and ∠B = 70°. Find the exterior angle in two ways.
    Show answer180° − 70° = 110°, and ∠A + ∠C = 50° + 60° = 110°. Tear the corners to watch A and C fill it exactly.
  5. Make all three angles equal. What is each one?
    Show answer60°, because 180° ÷ 3 = 60°. This is an equilateral triangle.

📖 Key terms

  • Interior angle: an angle inside the triangle, at one of its corners.
  • Exterior angle: the angle between one side and the extension of the next side.
  • Interior opposite angles: the two interior angles not next to a given exterior angle.
