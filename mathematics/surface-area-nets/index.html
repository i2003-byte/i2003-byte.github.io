📘 Learn the science

💡 The big idea

The surface area of a solid is the total area of all its faces: how much paper wraps a gift box, how much paint covers a wall, how much steel sheet makes a tank. A good way to find it is to cut the solid open along some edges and lay it flat. The flat shape is called its net, and its area is the surface area.

A cuboid unfolds into six rectangles that come in three equal pairs (top and bottom, front and back, left and right). A cylinder unfolds into a rectangle and two circles: the curved surface rolls out into a rectangle whose length is the distance round the circle, 2πr, and whose width is the height h.

🧮 Formulas

Cuboid: total surface area = 2(lb + bh + hl)

l = length, b = breadth, h = height. Each pair of equal faces gives one term, counted twice. A box with no lid has lb + 2(bh + hl).

Cube: total surface area = 6a²

All six faces are squares of side a. Doubling a makes every face 4 times bigger, so the area becomes 4 times bigger too.

Lateral surface of a cuboid = 2h(l + b)

Only the four side faces (the walls of a room): the perimeter of the base times the height.

Cylinder: total surface area = 2πrh + 2πr² = 2πr(r + h)

Curved surface 2πr × h, plus two circles of area πr² each. An open cylinder (a bucket or a drum without a lid) has 2πrh + πr². Textbook sums often use π = 22/7; the simulation uses the exact π, so its answers can differ in the last digit.

Room: area to paint = 2h(l + b) + lb − doors − windows

Walls plus the ceiling; the floor is not painted.

Simplifications: the faces have no thickness, and the sheet for a tank needs no overlap for joints or waste. Every door is taken as 1 m × 2 m and every window as 1.2 m × 1.2 m. Paint is worked out for two coats, with one litre covering about 10 m² per coat (the real figure depends on the paint and the wall). The costs are only examples.

🌍 Real-world examples

  • Sweet boxes: a mithai shop orders flat card nets that fold into boxes; the card needed per box is the box's surface area plus small flaps for gluing.
  • Painting a house before Diwali: painters measure the walls and ceiling of each room in square metres, subtract the doors and windows, and charge a rate per m².
  • Rooftop water tanks and oil drums are cylinders. The steel or plastic needed to make one is its total surface area.
  • A label on a can of paint or a tin of ghee is exactly the curved surface of the cylinder, a rectangle 2πr long.

🎯 Try this

  1. Keep the cuboid at 8 cm × 5 cm × 4 cm and unfold it. Find the area of each pair of faces and the total.
    Show answerTop and bottom 40 cm² each, front and back 32 cm² each, left and right 20 cm² each. Total 2(40 + 20 + 32) = 184 cm².
  2. Now switch off “Closed top (lid)”. How much card does the open box need?
    Show answer184 − 40 = 144 cm². The net has only five rectangles.
  3. Choose the cube with side 5 cm, then make the side 10 cm. What happens to the surface area?
    Show answerIt goes from 150 cm² to 600 cm²: 4 times bigger, because every face is 2 × 2 = 4 times bigger.
  4. Choose the cylinder with r = 3.5 cm and h = 10 cm. How long is the rectangle in its net, and what is the total surface area?
    Show answerThe rectangle is 2πr ≈ 22 cm long. Curved surface ≈ 220 cm², two circles ≈ 77 cm², total ≈ 297 cm² (296.9 cm² with the exact π).
  5. Paint the 5 m × 4 m × 3 m room with one door and two windows at ₹30 per m². How much area, paint and money is needed?
    Show answerWalls 54 m² + ceiling 20 m² − door 2 m² − windows 2.88 m² = 69.12 m². Two coats need about 13.8 L of paint, and the cost is about ₹2,074.

📖 Key terms

  • Net: a flat shape that folds up into a solid.
  • Total surface area: the sum of the areas of all the faces of a solid.
  • Lateral surface area: the area of the side faces only, leaving out the top and the bottom.
  • Curved surface: the round side of a cylinder; it unrolls into a rectangle.
