# SimLab Roadmap

> The living plan for SimLab. AI agents work through it automatically; see **AGENTS.md → Roadmap workflow**.
> Humans can edit anything here at any time: reorder, delete or add items. Agents follow whatever is written.

## Audience & scope
- **Students:** Class 7–12, India (CBSE / NCERT syllabus).
- **Subjects:** Physics first, then Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology (and Astronomy where the syllabus touches it).
- **Approved new subjects** (added automatically when their topics come up): Economics, Geography, Computer Science.
- **Out of scope for now:** languages.
- **Priority:** topics that are most taught and hardest to picture in a textbook, lower classes first.
- **Style:**
  - simple English
  - Indian contexts (₹, cricket, monsoon, trains, festivals, jal tarang…)
  - SI units
  - every simulation has a Learn panel with Try-this questions
- **Copyright:** use syllabus topic names only as structure. Write all explanations in our own words; never copy textbook text or images.
- **Approval:** **auto-approve.** Any topic that is part of the Class 7–12 India syllabus may go straight into 📋 Next. Out-of-scope ideas go to 💡 Proposed for a human to decide.

## 📋 Next (auto-approved; take items from the top. Each item plans ~3 simulations; each run builds 4)
- [ ] **Class 8 · Chemistry · Combustion and flame.** Branch `reactions`. Sims: the fire triangle (fuel, oxygen, ignition temperature; put out a fire by removing one); zones of a candle flame (hottest outer zone, unburnt wax vapour); fuel efficiency and calorific value of common fuels (wood, coal, LPG, CNG).
- [ ] **Class 9 · Chemistry · Atoms and molecules.** Branch `atoms`. Sims: law of conservation of mass and constant proportions (burn magnesium in a closed flask, water always 1 : 8 by mass); build a formula from valencies (criss-cross: Al₂O₃, CaCl₂, Na₂CO₃) with molecular mass; the mole (count particles by weighing: 1 mol of carbon, water and salt side by side, 6.022 × 10²³).
- [ ] **Class 12 · Physics · Electromagnetic induction.** Branch `electricity`. Sims: Faraday's law (move a magnet through a coil, galvanometer kick ∝ rate of change of flux); Lenz's law (falling magnet in a copper tube, eddy-current braking); self and mutual induction with a transformer (turns ratio, step-up/step-down, power lines).
- [ ] **Class 10 · Mathematics · Coordinate geometry.** Subject `mathematics` (new branch `coordinate-geometry`). Sims: distance formula (drag two points, the right triangle and Pythagoras; is it a square, a rectangle or a right triangle?); section formula (a point dividing a segment in m : n, midpoint, centroid); area of a triangle from coordinates (shoelace, collinear points give 0).
- [ ] **Class 11 · Physics · Mechanical properties of fluids.** Branch `mechanics`. Sims: Bernoulli's principle (water speeds up and pressure drops in a narrow pipe; Venturi meter, aeroplane wing lift, a fast train pulling paper towards it); viscosity and terminal velocity (steel balls falling in glycerine, Stokes' law, raindrops); surface tension and capillary rise (water in thin tubes, h = 2T cos θ/(rρg), mercury dips, insects on water).
- [ ] **Class 10 · Biology · Life processes.** Branch `cells` or a new `human-body` branch. Sims: the double circulation of the heart (four chambers, valves, oxygen-rich and oxygen-poor blood, pulse rate); digestion along the alimentary canal (enzymes acting on starch, protein and fat step by step); the kidney nephron (filtration and reabsorption, why we drink more water in summer).
