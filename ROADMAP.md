# SimLab Roadmap

> The living plan for SimLab. AI agents work through it automatically; see **AGENTS.md → Roadmap workflow**.
> Humans can edit anything here at any time: reorder, delete or add items. Agents follow whatever is written.

## Audience & scope
- **Students:** Class 7–12, India (CBSE / NCERT syllabus).
- **Subjects:** Physics first, then Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology (and Astronomy where the syllabus touches it).
- **Approved new subjects** (added automatically when their topics come up): Economics, Geography, Computer Science.
- **Out of scope for now:** languages.
- **Priority:** topics that are most taught and hardest to picture in a textbook, lower classes first.
- **Style:**
  - simple English
  - Indian contexts (₹, cricket, monsoon, trains, festivals, jal tarang…)
  - SI units
  - every simulation has a Learn panel with Try-this questions
- **Copyright:** use syllabus topic names only as structure. Write all explanations in our own words; never copy textbook text or images.
- **Approval:** **auto-approve.** Any topic that is part of the Class 7–12 India syllabus may go straight into 📋 Next. Out-of-scope ideas go to 💡 Proposed for a human to decide.

## 📋 Next (auto-approved; take items from the top. Each item plans ~3 simulations; each run builds 4)
- [ ] **Class 11 · Mathematics · Sets and Venn diagrams.** New branch `sets`. Sims: build sets by dragging items into circles (a class's cricket and football players; roster and set-builder form, subsets, empty and universal set); Venn operations (union, intersection, difference and complement shaded as you pick them, n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A ∩ B) checked with counts); survey problems (a colony survey of who reads Hindi and English newspapers, filling a three-set Venn diagram region by region).
- [ ] **Class 10 · Mathematics · Quadratic equations.** Branch `functions`. Sims: the parabola y = ax² + bx + c (roots where it meets the x-axis, the discriminant deciding two, one or no real roots, the vertex); completing the square with algebra tiles (x², x and unit tiles rearranged into a square, leading to the quadratic formula); quadratic word problems (a rectangular plot of given area and perimeter, a train's speed from a time difference, a ball thrown up).
- [ ] **Class 12 · Physics · Nuclei: radioactivity and half-life.** New branch `modern`. Sims: radioactive decay with many nuclei (random decays, half-life, N = N₀e^(−λt), dice model); carbon-14 dating (age of a wooden object or bone from its remaining C-14); binding energy, fission and chain reactions (U-235 splitting, neutrons, critical mass, controlled vs uncontrolled, as in Indian reactors at Tarapur and Kudankulam).
- [ ] **Class 11 · Chemistry · Equilibrium.** Branch `reactions`. Sims: dynamic equilibrium (molecules reacting forwards and backwards until the two rates match; same end point from either side); Le Chatelier's principle (N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃: add or remove gases, change pressure and temperature, Haber process conditions); ionisation of water and buffers (pH of strong and weak acids, how a buffer resists added acid or base, blood pH).
- [ ] **Class 8 · Geography · Resources: land, soil and water.** New branch `resources`. Sims: soil erosion and conservation (rain on a bare slope vs grass, terraces, contour ploughing and shelter belts; soil washed away per year); rooftop rainwater harvesting (roof area × rainfall = litres saved, tank size for a Chennai or Jaipur house through the year); groundwater and the water table (rain soaking in, wells and borewells pumping, the table falling in a dry year and recharge pits).
- [ ] **Class 10 · Biology · Control and coordination.** Branch `human-body`. Sims: reflex arc (touch a hot tawa: receptor → sensory neuron → spinal cord → motor neuron → muscle, timed against a thought-out response); plant movements (phototropism towards a window, geotropism of roots and shoot, touch-me-not leaves folding); hormones and feedback (insulin and blood sugar after a meal, adrenaline in a fright, thyroxine and iodised salt).
