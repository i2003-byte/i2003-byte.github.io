📘 Learn the science

💡 The big idea

Humans can hear sounds with frequencies from about 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz — these are audible sounds. Sounds below 20 Hz are infrasonic (infrasound) and sounds above 20,000 Hz are ultrasonic (ultrasound). Many animals hear sounds we cannot: dogs hear high-pitched whistles, bats use ultrasound to find their way, and elephants communicate with low infrasonic rumbles.

🧮 Key formulas & rules

Infrasonic: below 20 Hz Audible (humans): 20 Hz – 20,000 Hz Ultrasonic: above 20,000 Hz (20 kHz)

🌍 Real-world examples

  • Dog whistles are silent to us but dogs come running
  • Bats and dolphins use ultrasound echoes to find food (echolocation)
  • Doctors use ultrasound scans to see inside the body
  • Some animals sense the infrasound of earthquakes before people do

📖 Key terms

  • Audible — Sound humans can hear: 20 Hz – 20,000 Hz.
  • Infrasonic — Sound below 20 Hz.
  • Ultrasonic — Sound above 20,000 Hz.
