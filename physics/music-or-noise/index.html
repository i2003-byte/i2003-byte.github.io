📘 Learn the science

💡 The big idea

Sounds that are pleasant to hear, like a flute or a song, are musical sounds. Their waves have a regular pattern that repeats again and again. Unpleasant sounds, like traffic or a drill, are called noise. Their waves are irregular — jumbled, with no repeating pattern. (Even music can become noise if it is played too loudly!)

🧮 Key formulas & rules

Regular, repeating wave → musical sound Irregular wave → noise

🌍 Real-world examples

  • A harmonium, sitar or flute produces musical sounds
  • Honking horns, construction and crowds produce noise
  • Very loud music from a loudspeaker can become noise for neighbours

📖 Key terms

  • Musical sound — A pleasant sound with a regular wave pattern.
  • Noise — An unpleasant sound with an irregular wave pattern.
