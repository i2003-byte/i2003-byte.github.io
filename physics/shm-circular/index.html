📘 Learn the science

💡 The big idea

Shine a light from above on a ball going round a circle at a steady speed. Its shadow on the floor slides back and forth along a straight line, fast in the middle and slow at the ends. That back-and-forth motion is exactly simple harmonic motion (SHM), the same motion as a block on a spring.

This link is very useful: the radius of the circle is the amplitude, the time for one turn is the period, and the angle of the point is the phase. Everything about SHM can be read off the circle.

🧮 Key formulas & rules

θ = ωt + φ, ω = 2π ÷ T = 2πf x = A cos(ωt + φ) v = −Aω sin(ωt + φ) a = −ω² x

The point P has speed Aω along the tangent and a centripetal acceleration Aω² towards the centre. The shadow only gets the horizontal part of each. So vmax = Aω (at x = 0) and amax = Aω² (at x = ±A).

a = −ω²x is the test for SHM: the acceleration is proportional to the displacement and points the other way, back to the centre. On the graph, v is a quarter cycle ahead of x, and a is exactly opposite to x.

🌍 Real-world examples

  • In a sewing machine or a car engine, a wheel turning in a circle drives a needle or piston up and down: circular motion becomes nearly simple harmonic motion.
  • Seen edge-on from Earth, Jupiter's moons appear to swing from side to side of the planet in SHM, because they really go round it in near-circles.
  • AC electricity is described with the same rotating picture (phasors) in Class 12.

🎯 Try this

  1. With A = 10 cm and T = 3 s, find ω, the top speed and the biggest acceleration of the shadow.
    Show answerω = 2π ÷ 3 ≈ 2.09 rad/s. vmax = Aω ≈ 20.9 cm/s, at the centre. amax = Aω² ≈ 43.9 cm/s², at the ends.
  2. Pause when P is at the top of the circle (θ = 90°). Where is the shadow, and what are its v and a?
    Show answerThe shadow is at x = 0. P's velocity points straight left, so the shadow moves at the full speed Aω. P's acceleration points straight down, which has no horizontal part, so the shadow's acceleration is zero.
  3. Set the phase φ to 90°. How does the start change?
    Show answerNow P starts at the top, so the shadow starts at the centre moving left, instead of at the right end at rest. The motion is the same, just shifted by a quarter period.
  4. Look at the graph. Which curve reaches its peak first, x or v?
    Show answerv peaks a quarter period before x does: v leads x by 90°. a is always the mirror image of x (a = −ω²x).

📖 Key terms

  • Reference circle: the circle whose shadow gives the SHM.
  • Angular frequency (ω): the angle turned per second, in rad/s.
  • Phase: the angle ωt + φ that tells where in its cycle the motion is; φ is the starting phase.
