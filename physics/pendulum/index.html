📘 Learn the science

💡 The big idea

Pull the bob aside and gravity pulls it back towards the lowest point. It overshoots, swings up the other side and repeats: an oscillation. Energy keeps changing form — potential energy at the ends, kinetic energy at the bottom. With damping (air friction) the swings slowly die away as energy turns into heat.

🧮 Key formulas & rules

T₀ = 2π √(L / g)

period for small swings — independent of mass and (almost) of amplitude

θ″ = −(g/L) sin θ − b θ′

the full equation the simulation solves

KE = ½ m v², PE = m g L (1 − cos θ)

energy of the bob

🌍 Real-world examples

  • Grandfather clocks keep time using a pendulum
  • Children on a swing
  • A wrecking ball
  • Foucault's pendulum shows the Earth rotating

🎯 Try this

  1. Make the pendulum 4 times longer. What happens to the period?
    Show answerIt doubles, because T ∝ √L.
  2. Change the mass from 1 kg to 10 kg. Does the period change?
    Show answerNo — the period does not depend on mass.
  3. Compare the small-angle and exact periods at 10° and at 90°.
    Show answerAt 10° they are nearly equal; at 90° the real period is about 18% longer.
  4. Take the pendulum to the Moon. Is it faster or slower?
    Show answerSlower — weaker gravity gives a longer period (about 2.5× longer).
