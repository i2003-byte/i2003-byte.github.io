💡 The big idea

Noise pollution is the presence of unwanted, excessive sound in the surroundings. It can cause lack of sleep, high blood pressure, anxiety and even hearing loss. We can reduce it by controlling noise at the source (silencers, lower volume, no horns), along its path (trees, walls) and by keeping noisy activities away from where people live, study or heal.

🧮 Key formulas & rules

Energy of sounds adds up: two equal sources → +3 dB Every doubling of distance → about −6 dB Goals: hospital ≤ 50 dB, school ≤ 55 dB, homes ≤ 55 dB

🌍 Real-world examples

  • Silencers on vehicle exhausts
  • Rows of trees planted along highways
  • Sound barriers beside flyovers and railway lines
  • Industries located away from residential areas
  • “No horn” signs near hospitals and schools

📖 Key terms

  • Noise pollution — Unwanted, excessive sound that harms health.
  • Silencer — A device fitted to exhausts to reduce engine noise.
  • Soundproof — Built to stop sound passing through.
